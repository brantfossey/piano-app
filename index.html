    Getting started

    1. Connect the Yamaha CVP's USB TO HOST port (or a USB-MIDI adapter on its MIDI OUT) to the computer/phone. Use Chrome or Edge on Windows, or Chrome on Android (USB-OTG adapter).
    2. Click Connect MIDI, allow access, then choose the input. If you hear doubled notes or the CVP's style accompaniment confuses the app, set Ch to channel 1.
    3. No piano handy? Click the on-screen keys, or use the computer keyboard: Z S X D C V… = C3 upwards, Q 2 W 3 E R… = C4 upwards.

    Play-along

    Wait for me stops on each note/chord until you play it. Steady tempo runs with a metronome and scores timing (perfect ≤60 ms, good ≤120 ms) and accuracy. Melody + chord symbols is the chord-fluency mode: read the melody with your right hand and play the chord named above the staff with your left hand in any voicing or inversion.

    Lyrics & Chords

    A chord sheet like Ultimate Guitar: the chords sit above the words. Press Follow along while the song plays in the room (the app doesn't listen to it). The first chord is highlighted; play it on the piano (any inversion or voicing, extra notes OK) and the highlight moves to the next one. Use Back/Next (or the ← → keys) if you miss one, Restart to start over, or click any chord to jump there. Songs without written lyrics show the melody's syllables, or just a chord chart.

    Paste chord sheet: in Library & Editor → “Optional lyrics & chords”, paste a text chord sheet copied from a chords app (chords on one line, the words on the line below, [Verse 1] headers) and press Convert pasted chord sheet. Each chord is placed on the syllable under it, chord-only lines (intro, solo) stay chord lines, and the result is saved on the song you are editing, in this browser only. With simplify unsupported chords on, chords the app doesn't know are replaced by the closest one it does (Am7(#5) → Am7, Bb7sus2 → Bb7sus4). Use this to add the words to a chords-only song such as “Nothing's Gonna Change My Love For You”.

    Chord names (top bar) sets how inversions are shown everywhere: slash chords (A/C#), the chord name with a small “1st inversion”, or just the chord name.

    Arrangements

    Pick a style (pop ballad, ragtime stride, gospel/hymn, country) and a left-hand pattern (block, broken, stride). The generator builds both hands from the melody and chord symbols. Save it into the song if you like it.

    Getting songs in

    Type them in the editor, enter them note by note from the piano (step entry), or give sheet music to an AI assistant along with the prompt from Copy AI prompt, then import the JSON it writes. The format is documented in README.md.

    Your library is stored in this browser (IndexedDB). Export regularly to keep a backup.
